## 📊 Facebook vs AdWords Campaign Effectiveness – ROI Optimization Analysis

### 🧩 Problem Statement
A marketing agency aimed to determine which ad platform—**Facebook or AdWords**—delivers better performance in terms of **clicks**, **conversions**, and **cost-effectiveness**. With 365 days of campaign data from 2019, the goal was to analyze which platform maximizes **ROI** and provides **data-driven insights** for better budget allocation.

### ✅ Solution Overview
Using Python and data analysis libraries (`pandas`, `seaborn`, `matplotlib`, `scikit-learn`), I performed:

- 📌 **Descriptive analysis** to compare daily clicks and conversions across platforms.
- 📊 **Distribution visualization** (histograms & scatterplots) to identify outliers and patterns.
- 🔗 **Correlation analysis** showing a **strong positive relationship (r = 0.87)** between Facebook ad clicks and conversions.
- 🧪 **Hypothesis testing** confirming Facebook's higher average conversions with a statistically significant **t-test (p ≈ 0)**.
- 📈 **Regression modeling** to predict Facebook conversions based on ad clicks with **R² = 76.35%**.
- 📅 **Time-series insights** identifying **conversion trends** by day of the week and month.
- 💰 **Cost-effectiveness (CPC) analysis**, showing strategic windows (e.g., May & Nov) for maximizing ROI.
- ⚖️ **Equilibrium relationship testing** indicating long-term stability between **spend and conversions**.

> 🔍 **Conclusion:** The findings clearly showed **Facebook outperformed AdWords** in both conversion rate and cost-efficiency, enabling the business to **reallocate budget more effectively for higher ROI**.
